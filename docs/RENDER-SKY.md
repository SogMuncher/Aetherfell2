# Rendering: sky, air, clouds, fog (part of docs/RENDER.md)

- The air is a stack of LOD levels of cubic cells round the eye (`airLevels.ts` plans, sizes in `SkyDef.air`), each twice as wide as the one below, built whole on the GPU (`gpu/gpuAirFill.ts`): the GLSL there is `airColumn` (content/skyField.ts), the same rule rain, in-cloud fog and the sim read; layer patterns are uploaded once, each layer's edge (`layerEdge`) set per frame from the weather. A new cloud/mist kind is a new layer in `content/sky.ts`, nothing else.
- Planner order: empty level, then one the eye left (finest first), then the oldest after `air.refresh` × (k + 1) s; at most `air.perFrame` a frame. Cells sit in the wind's drifting frame, so a rebuild matches the old level wherever both reach (no pops, no fade slots). The march (`gpu/gpuClouds.ts`) walks cells nearest first; each level blends smoothly into the next over the second half of each doubling (no dither). Each clump is one solid shape: entry face sets its look, plus a 4-sample walk toward the sun (`air.self`) for self-shadow and a silver edge against the sun.
- Damp air (`content/airWet.ts`): coarse land moisture (600 m cells, layer `wet`) and closeness of open water (`SkyDef.damp`, layer `water`; the sim wires `waterSpot` edge distance via `setAirWater`), so mist gathers over rivers and lakes. The GPU reads both from wrapped byte grids (`airGrids.ts` WrapGrid, filled nearest first within a time budget).
- Cloud shadows (`gpu/gpuCloudShade.ts`, `SkyDef.shade`): two maps round the eye (near/far texel size) walked up toward the light through the air cells' shadow share (layer `shadow`); the light pass reads `cloudShade(p)` projecting p along the light. Sun rays (`gpu/gpuGodRays.ts`, `SkyDef.rays`): half-res radial walk toward the sun over open sky (depth = sky, minus cloud cover), added in the sun colour.
- All air moves with the wind (one drifting frame, no per-layer speed). Smoke is the live level of the air (`airSmoke.ts`, `SkyDef.smoke`): the matter state look `smoke` (fire) feeds the one 1 m cell above each fire; it rises slowly, spreads a little sideways and thins in steps, only where smoke is, so one fire makes a thin column and many fires a cloud.
- Fog (`env.ts`): the fog colour is the sky's horizon colour, or the cloud you stand in (shorter sight, cloud colour); clouds use `Env.cloudLit` (same light as the overcast sky) and haze over `fogDist × sky.haze`.
- Floating air motes (`gpu/gpuAir.ts`) drift with their `AirDef.drift` plus the weather wind under open sky. Speed lines (`gpu/gpuSpeedLines.ts`) start at 7 m/s, full at 15.
